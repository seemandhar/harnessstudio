- **H2 deeper re-close** (`rules.H2Trigger`, `rules.Reclose`): after the first empty close before any lifted carry
  (>= 3 close commands, width <= 4 mm within 6 steps), open 3 steps, descend `a_z = -0.5` until -3 cm / 8 steps /
  contact (realized dz > -1 mm), hand back. At most 2 fires; the 2nd needs a >= 2 cm rise. Sham: ascend 3 cm.
- **H1 clamp-lift** (`rules.H1Trigger`, `rules.ClampLift`): when the policy proposes the open that would release a
  settled (>= 10 steps, > 1 cm, settled 0.5 mm), never-lifted, non-placement, non-twist clamp, veto it and lift
  `a_z = +0.5` up to 12 steps / +6 cm; after 4 steps abort (execute the vetoed open) if the rise < 1 cm or width <= 4 mm.
  At most 3 fires, one per close run. Sham: veto-only (hold closed 12 steps).
- **Switches** (`params.modules`): `h2`, `h1`, `h3` (freeze nudge; off in gpp-core) = on | sham | off; `ge` = greedy-escape's
  frozen StallMonitor + SCALE windows (imported read-only from `hs_greedy_escape`; must equal its card exactly).
- **Entry points** (cards generated by `scripts/make_cards.py` from one frozen constant set): `gpp-core`, `gpp-core-sham`,
